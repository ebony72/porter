Method. Each cell is one deterministic forward pass (selection=False) from PORTER’s own initial layout at seed 0, reporting EPR pairs consumed, measured against the current router. t/o means no result inside a 300 s per-route guard. GHZ and Graphstate are identical at every horizon and are marked not sensitive.
Why one set. The retired rule chose {4,8,16} for circuits whose interaction density ρ reached 0.80 and {8,16,128} otherwise — a threshold fitted on these same 48 instances. The union of those two sets matches it: parity on the main suite, 0.2% better pooled over all 48, and identical on every main instance once backward refinement runs on top. ρ is still shown, and now decides nothing. Other four-value sets do about as well — {4,16,32,128}, {4,16,48,128} and {4,16,64,128} all land within a percent — so the result does not turn on these particular numbers.
What it costs. Four routes rather than three: 1.6 × the compile time for a single pass and 1.3 × with refinement, in exchange for removing the one fitted rule in PORTER’s own protocol.
