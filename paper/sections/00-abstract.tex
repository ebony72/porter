\begin{abstract}
A distributed quantum computer executes a circuit across multiple small
cores connected by entanglement-distribution links\chg{;} under the cost
model considered here, inter-core communication consumes
Einstein--Podolsky--Rosen (EPR) pairs, which the compiler
minimises. Choosing an inter-core action is coupled to local routing:
\chg{a router that scores} an action together with the preparation
\chg{its current layout demands can} favour an easy-to-prepare action
\chg{over} one that provides greater routing progress.
We present \PORTER{}, a deterministic, plan-committed router
\chg{that represents} each
candidate inter-core action as a \chg{\emph{plan}: its} communication
primitive, the gates it can discharge, and its readiness conditions. It
ranks plans by progress, uses preparation cost only to break ties, commits
to one, and then generates the local SWAPs needed to execute it.
\chg{It} supports \TeleData{}, \TeleGate{}, and same-control
\TeleGatePacket{} operations, \chg{and adds} capacity relief, a
deterministic initial layout, and a fixed four-horizon teleport-lookahead
portfolio.
On MQT-Bench suites with 25, 64, and 80 logical qubits, \PORTER{} uses
$59.7\%$, $44.9\%$, and $68.8\%$ fewer EPR pairs in geometric mean than
\TeleSABRE{} \chg{over the instances both complete, and completes all
24 main-suite instances to \TeleSABRE{}'s 17. Against \dSABRE{} it is}
competitive rather than uniformly superior under
the respective native protocols\chg{, using} $0.99\times$ \dSABRE{}'s
pooled EPR at $1.20\times$ its complete compile time\chg{, and it uses
$0.69\times$ \dSABRE{}'s pairs on a} 100--360-qubit QFT and QPEexact
scale-up series. \chg{The two prove complementary: taking the lower count
of the two per instance uses $9.4\%$ fewer pairs than \dSABRE{} alone.}
Ablations show that capacity relief \chg{earns its place on completion
rather than cost, five of nine 80-qubit circuits failing without it}. The
implementation, \chg{harness}, replay verifier, and raw results are
\chg{public}.
\end{abstract}
